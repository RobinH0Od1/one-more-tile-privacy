One More Tile Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 September 2026

One More Tile is a local puzzle game published by Robin. This policy describes the information handled by the Android version of the app.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, best scores, daily-puzzle history, and sound, haptic, and local ad-frequency preferences on your device. This data is not sent to the publisher and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The game may show occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Ads are provided by Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and ad availability, Google may process information such as your IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), ad or device identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostics.

Google may use this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this SDK traffic in transit. For more information, see Google's Google Play data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's consent dialog is shown before ads are requested. You can revisit available privacy choices from the game's Settings screen.

Sharing

The publisher does not sell or independently share your locally stored game progress. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is transmitted to Google and its advertising partners as described in Google's documentation and policies.

Children's privacy

One More Tile does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system or direct mechanism for submitting personal information.

Your choices and questions

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For questions or privacy requests, contact the developer using the support email shown on the One More Tile Google Play listing.

This policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
